# QRslice — Café Owner Onboarding Script

> Use this when talking to a restaurant/café owner (in person, phone, or WhatsApp).
> Keep it simple. Speak their language. Don't sound like software.
> Updated for the **2-plan SaaS**: Basic ₹399 / Pro ₹799, 30-day free trial, self-signup.

---

## 1. The Hook (open friendly, not salesy)

"Sir/Madam, aapki café mein customer order kaise dete hain? Waiter bulana,
galti se galat order jana, bill banane mein der — ye sab hota hai rozana, hai na?

Main ek chhota sa system laaya hoon jo aapke customer ko **direct table par QR
scan karke order dene deta hai** — bina kisi app download ke. Aur aapko **billing +
kitchen screen** milta hai. Suniye 2 minute, free hai try karne ka."

---

## 2. The Problem (they should nod)

"Dekhiye aajkal ke customer ko wait nahi karna padta:
- Waiter busy ho toh order late hota hai
- Handwritten order mein galti ho jati hai
- Peak time mein bill banane mein line lagti hai
- Aur GST bill proper banana mein tension hoti hai"

---

## 3. The Solution — what QRslice does

"Hamara naam hai **QRslice** — ekdum self-serve SaaS. Aap khud 2 minute mein
apni café bana lete hain, humare paas account banana padta hai.

1. **Customer QR Order** — Har table par QR. Customer scan kare, menu dikhai de,
   order direct kitchen mein chala jaye. App nahi chahiye.
2. **KDS (Kitchen Display)** — Chef ko screen par order saaf dikhai de, 'ready'
   dabaye, customer ko pata chal jaye. *(Pro plan mein)*
3. **POS + GST Bill** — Counter par ek tap se bill. Proper GST receipt — shop name,
   address, GSTIN, CGST/SGST alag-alag, HSN, amount-in-words — thermal printer par.

Matlab: **order fast, galti kam, bill clean.**"

---

## 4. How it works for THEM (super simple, self-serve)

"Bhaut easy hai — aap khud kar lenge:
- `qrslice.app/onboarding` kholein → café name + GST details bharein
- Plan chunein (Basic ya Pro) + email/password
- Menu starter template milta hai, table ka QR auto-generate ho jata hai
- 10 minute ka setup, phir QR print kar frame rakh dijiye"

---

## 5. The Price — be clear, no confusion (2 plans)

"Suniye pricing bilkul clear hai, do plan hain:

🟡 **Basic — ₹399/month**
   - 10 table tak, 50 menu item tak
   - Customer QR order + counter POS + GST bill

🟢 **Pro — ₹799/month**
   - Unlimited table & item
   - + KDS kitchen screen + apna branding (logo/rang) + analytics + multi-location

🎁 **Dono mein pehla 30 din BILKUL FREE** — trial. Card nahi, commitment nahi.
   Trial khatam hone par aapko bas ₹399 ya ₹799 bharne hain (Razorpay se, auto monthly).
   Agar nahi chahiye — **chhod dijiye, koi lock-in nahi, koi jhook nahi.**

Matlab risk humara, faida aapka."

---

## 6. Handle their objections (keep ready)

| Unka sawal | Aapka jawaab |
|---|---|
| "Mujhe computer nahi aata" | "Phone chalana aata hai? Bas utna kaafi. 2 minute mein khud sign-up kar lenge." |
| "Customers app download karenge?" | "Nahi. Sirf camera se QR scan. WhatsApp jaise hi." |
| "Internet chala gaya toh?" | "Order local save hota hai, wapas aane par sync ho jata hai." |
| "₹799 zyada hai" | "Wo Pro hai. Basic sirf ₹399 hai. Ek din ki 2-3 galat order ya 1 ghante waiter bachti se nikal aata hai. Upar se 30 din free." |
| "KDS chahiye?" | "Wo Pro ₹799 mein hai. Basic mein counter POS + QR order milta hai. Badme upgrade kar lenge." |
| "Puranai machine se kaam chal raha" | "Wahi rakhiye. QRslice upar se add hota hai, replace nahi karta." |
| "30 din baad kya?" | "Trial khatam par order band ho jayega, lekin admin khula rahega. ₹399/₹799 bharo toh chalta rahega, nahi toh chhod do." |

---

## 7. Close (soft, with a next step)

"Toh karte hain aise — aap khud `qrslice.app/onboarding` kholiye, café banaiye,
30 din free try kariye. Customer khush hone lage toh Basic ya Pro continue karna,
nahi toh koi baat nahi.

Chalo phone mein abhi shuru karte hain? 2 minute."
